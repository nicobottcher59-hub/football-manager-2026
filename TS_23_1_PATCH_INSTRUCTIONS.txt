TS-23.1 - Youth Academy UI, paid scouts and poachable prospects
INCREMENTAL UPDATE - not a full game

Required base: TS-23 Youth Academy & Newgens installed. No Node or npm.

1. Stop INICIAR_INTERFAZ_WINDOWS.bat and close the running Python server.
2. Make a backup of the complete game folder and your full SQLite career save.
3. Extract this ZIP into the project root (the folder with
   INICIAR_INTERFAZ_WINDOWS.bat). Preserve folders and replace files.
4. Run: python tools/verify_ts231_academy_package.py
5. Run: python -m pytest -ra tests/test_ts_23_1_academy_polish.py
6. Start with INICIAR_INTERFAZ_WINDOWS.bat, press Ctrl+F5 and open Youth Academy.

NEW CAREERS: Youth scouts start EMPTY. You can hire up to 3 scouts from
an offer list with a signing fee and monthly wages. The recruitment allocation
is provisional and separate from the as-yet-unimplemented club finance system.

EXISTING TS-23 SAVES: the original three scouts are preserved as legacy free
staff with £0 monthly wage. No scouting expenses are backdated. You may end
those agreements or continue using them. The new youth allocation starts at
£500,000 and is replenished annually in amounts defined by club division.

Following a discovered prospect does not reserve or sign them. From 30 days
after discovery, AI clubs can sometimes sign unsigned candidates. They enter
the AI's real U17 roster with the same persistent player ID. A short record
of lost prospects remains visible.

The complete SQLite career save upgrades to version 11 when saved. Loading
alone does not overwrite the previous save file. Keep your original backup.
Use the full SQLite save, not the results-only JSON export.
