TS-UI-03.02 - Training automation and individual player workloads

BASE REQUIRED: TS-UI-03.01 (which in turn requires Squad TS-UI-02.14).
No separate photo-only patch is required; the requested photo is included.

1. Back up your career save folder before changing game files. This patch
   upgrades the manager-graph save schema from 22 to 23; older saves migrate
   automatically when loaded by the new version.
2. Stop the Python game server.
3. Open the root of football-manager-2026 (the folder with frontend/ and src/).
4. Extract this ZIP directly there, KEEPING internal subfolders. Replace files.
5. Restart: PYTHONPATH=src python3 -m football_manager.ui_shell
6. Open Training and hard-refresh (Ctrl+F5).
7. Set one group or player to Automatic, or click Apply to all groups.
   Adjust the four linked cutoffs and click Save automatic plans.
8. Under Individual player training, choose a player and a Personal workload;
   use Follow squad group to clear the exception. Development focus is shared
   with Squad and applies at the next monthly review.

Training priority: individual choice > squad group > physical safety cap.
When automatic energy and performance conditions both apply, energy first.
The two old physical protection cutoffs still work exactly as before.

Do not run npx vite; use the Python UI shell above.
