TS-24.5 - Autonomous English Transfer Market and Formation-Aware Squad Planning
INCREMENTAL PATCH - NOT THE FULL GAME

REQUIRED BASE: TS-24.4 installed, including all earlier incremental updates.
No Node, npm, internet connection or JavaScript build step is required.

WINDOWS INSTALLATION
1. Close INICIAR_INTERFAZ_WINDOWS.bat and the Python game server.
2. Back up your entire project directory and the complete SQLite career save.
3. Extract this ZIP DIRECTLY into the project root (the folder containing
   INICIAR_INTERFAZ_WINDOWS.bat). Preserve relative paths and overwrite files.
   The ZIP contains no football-manager-game wrapper folder.
4. Run from the project folder:
   python tools/verify_ts245_ai_market_package.py
5. Optional with pytest installed:
   python -m pytest -q tests/test_ts_24_5_ai_squad_market.py
6. Run INICIAR_INTERFAZ_WINDOWS.bat and force-refresh the browser (Ctrl+F5).

WHAT CHANGES
- All English clubs use the existing main tactical formation to plan two
  complete elevens (starters and backups) plus a third GK when feasible.
- A versatile player cannot simultaneously fill two places in the planner.
- Shortages take priority over upgrading strong positions. AI clubs can sell
  backups when doing so will not remove needed starting coverage; low-depth
  goalkeeper sales are protected.
- AI-managed clubs buy and sell professionals between themselves in open
  windows, subject to transfer budget, cash, wage budget and player consent.
- Your managed club is NEVER bought from or sold to autonomously. Other clubs
  can submit offers for your players, but you retain the decision.
- AI trades preserve contracts, accounts, player IDs and career history.
- World transfer news appears under Scouting -> World transfers. Tactical
  squad-coverage summaries appear in Other Leagues -> club details.
- A cached opposing lineup is refreshed after an AI transfer changes its squad.

TRY IT IN GAME
1. Load your career; use Calendar to simulate from late August to September.
2. Go to Scouting -> World transfers: inspect the completed rival deals.
3. Go to Other Leagues -> choose a club: inspect starters/backup/goalkeeper
   squad coverage and any outstanding needs.
4. Continue through January for the next window and compare roster balances.
5. If a rival makes you an offer, review it under Scouting -> Offers received;
   no automatic sale happens to your club.

LIMITATIONS
- Initial transfer windows: January, July and August. This is a simplified
  English market calendar; detailed division-specific registration deadlines
  are planned separately.
- AI-to-AI transfers currently use a guaranteed up-front fee and agreed
  professional wages. User-initiated deals retain optional bonus and sell-on
  clauses. No instalments.
- A third goalkeeper is a TARGET, not a guaranteed free signing; the AI will
  not strip another club of its only reserve GK just to fill that target.
- Loans, free agents, exercised buyback options, broader world scouting and
  complete 5/10/20-season sporting balance remain future stages.

SAVES
- Graph and SQLite save format remain VERSION 15. No schema migration.
- Existing TS-24.4 full-career saves are compatible. Take backups anyway.
- Completed autonomous transfers persist in the regular SQLite career save.
