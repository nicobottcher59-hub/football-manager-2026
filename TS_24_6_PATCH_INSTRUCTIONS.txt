TS-24.6 - Loans, free agents and buyback clauses
INCREMENTAL UPDATE - not a full game

REQUIRED BASE: TS-24.5 already installed. No Node or npm needed.

INSTALLATION (Windows)
1. Close the game/server launched by INICIAR_INTERFAZ_WINDOWS.bat.
2. Back up your game folder and your FULL SQLite career save.
3. Extract this ZIP into the game's ROOT directory, which contains
   INICIAR_INTERFAZ_WINDOWS.bat. Preserve the included subfolders and
   replace files on request.
4. In PowerShell at that directory, run:
       python tools/verify_ts246_loans_package.py
5. Optionally run the focused regression tests (some can take minutes):
       python -m pytest -ra tests/test_ts_24_6_loans_free_agents.py
6. Run INICIAR_INTERFAZ_WINDOWS.bat and use Ctrl+F5 to reload the UI.

WHERE TO USE IT
- Scouting -> Loans & free agents.
- Loan out / Loan in: select a player and propose a 6-, 12- or 24-month loan.
- Choose the BORROWER's percentage of the existing wage and a credible
  playing-time role. Parent retains the employment contract. There are no
  installment payments.
- Optionally include a fixed future purchase price; this is NOT required.
  The borrowing manager may exercise it only in the last 90 days of the
  loan AND during a transfer window; the player must still consent.
- My loans: inspect active/completed agreements, playing time and the
  minutes target, recall eligible outgoing players, or buy with the option.
- Free agents: after contracts expire and the monthly review is run,
  eligible players can be signed directly with a professional contract.
- Buybacks: exercise valid buyback rights from earlier player sales.

GUARDRAILS / LIMITATIONS
- The lender cannot abandon its starting XI or second goalkeeper.
- An academy youth must first be promoted to the FIRST TEAM before loans.
- Playing-time promises are recorded and monitored; the game does not yet
  impose automatic sanctions on clubs missing a promise.
- AI clubs use vacant roles to sign some free agents; autonomous AI-to-AI
  loan offers are NOT part of this release.
- Salaries are prorated by actual active loan days. An unsigned player has
  no club wage bill until a new contract is signed.
- Contract expiry is reviewed monthly; some essential players can remain
  under interim paid retention until the club has safe coverage.
- A loan requires the parent professional contract to cover its full length.
- Temporary borrower registration and permanent parental contract are both
  saved. No player cloning. Duplicate monthly payments are prohibited.

SAVE FORMAT: graph/manager-state version 16 (previously 15). Existing v15
saves migrate forward on load and are only overwritten when you save again.
Keep your backup; previous builds may reject version 16.
