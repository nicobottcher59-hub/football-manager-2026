TS-24.4 — Player Consent, Agents & Completed Transfers
INCREMENTAL PATCH — NOT THE COMPLETE GAME

REQUIRED BASE: TS-24.3.1 Scouting & Negotiation Flow installed.
No Node, npm, internet, or new build step is required to play.

INSTALLATION ON WINDOWS
1. Close INICIAR_INTERFAZ_WINDOWS.bat and the Python game server.
2. Back up the entire game directory and the complete SQLite career save.
3. Extract the ZIP directly into the folder with INICIAR_INTERFAZ_WINDOWS.bat,
   keeping relative paths and allowing overwrite. The ZIP has no wrapper folder.
4. In PowerShell from that game folder, run:
   python tools/verify_ts244_transfers_package.py
5. Optional, with pytest installed:
   python -m pytest -q tests/test_ts_24_4_personal_transfers.py
   (This can take several minutes due full-world SQLite save/restore cases.)
6. Start INICIAR_INTERFAZ_WINDOWS.bat and refresh browser using Ctrl+F5.

TRY A TRANSFER
A. Scouting -> find professional player -> Make transfer offer. Alternatively:
   Other Leagues -> club -> expand a player -> Make transfer offer.
B. Reach club agreement via the existing offers and counteroffers.
C. In the same immersive view, negotiate salary per week, contract duration,
   squad role and optional signing-on fee with the player's agent.
D. The agent may agree, counter, or refuse due to level/ambition. You can
   accept a counteroffer. Completing the agreement updates clubs, wages,
   transfer budgets and squads immediately.
E. For an agreed offer received for your own player, open it and select
   'Ask player and agent to decide'. A successful sale settles the fee.
F. Check Squad, Finances and the buyer/seller club after completion.

TRANSFER ACCOUNTING
- The guaranteed transfer fee is paid immediately, with no instalments.
- Bonuses for appearances, goals, assists and promotion are registered;
  payments occur only after real milestones and at most once per clause.
- Negotiated future-sale share is paid on later resales (gross or profit).
- Buyback options remain recorded but cannot yet be exercised. The first
  release does NOT add a complete AI-vs-AI window market, free agents or loans.

SAVES
Full manager career graph and SQLite manager sections upgrade to VERSION 15.
Compatible version-14 saves migrate the new personal negotiation fields.
Loading alone does not overwrite a previous save. Saving in TS-24.4 will
produce a version-15 archive; older patches may not load that archive.
Retain the backup and always use the full SQLite career save.
