# TC-E1 — International Calendar Foundation (2026+)

Status: TC-E1.1 extends the FIFA reprogramming to the user-managed 2026/27 DEV world. The opening 15 August fixtures remain unchanged; future matchweeks are adjusted around FIFA club breaks. The implementation for generated later seasons remains enabled.

## Agreed behavior

- FIFA ordinary match windows are recurring, shared club-league breaks in the current game design.
- World Cup, EURO, Copa America, Asian Cup and AFCON reserve national-team **release periods**. **They do not automatically stop club leagues.** Future national-team call-ups will determine the specific absent club players. The registry does not itself invent call-ups or modify the current player lineup/simulation.
- Fixed **weeks**, not immutable exact dates: known published dates anchor the calendar; subsequent editions use clearly labelled **projections**, with explicit overrides available.
- **Normal fixture first, FIFA adjustment second:** league fixture opponents and natural weekly slots are produced *without* FIFA exclusions. A deterministic second scheduling pass moves a whole affected matchweek into an available date, preserving matchweek identity/order and a minimum three-day gap between league rounds. Original kickoff is retained on every moved fixture (`ScheduledFixture.original_kickoff`). Some neighboring rounds may also move when required by rest/available dates. Cup and staged competitions likewise keep the originally requested kickoff when moved from their reference window.
- The world runner's new FIFA-aware booking and reprogramming logic applies to generated worlds, staged/cup fixtures and explicit future postponements. Matches are not silently cancelled or dropped if the requested window cannot fit; the operation fails.
- The portable UI displays **only a discreet small globe icon** on ordinary FIFA days. The icon is informational, with a tooltip; it does not occupy a fixture row, hide results, or create a fake match. Selected fixtures moved to another day can show their original date in the side panel.

## Calendar data and sources

`src/football_manager/domain/international_calendar.py` contains immutable calendar events with IDs, date spans, sources (`official`, `projected`, `override`), impacts (`club_break`, `release_only`), and national-team release lead periods. It serializes to/from JSON-compatible dicts. `InternationalDutyRegistry` holds only explicitly supplied player call-ups; an early elimination can shorten a call-up.

- FIFA official men's 2026–2030 windows, including the combined September–October window: https://inside.fifa.com/tournament-organisation/international-match-calendars
- 2026 men's World Cup dates and player release: same FIFA schedule.
- AFC Asian Cup 2027: January 7 – February 5 (official): https://www.the-afc.com/en/national/afc_asian_cup.html/news/dates-for-afc-asian-cup-saudi-arabia-2027%E2%84%A2-recommended
- CAF AFCON 2027: June 19 – July 17. CAF has initiated bidding for editions 2028, 2032 and 2036; projected 2028+ dates are game templates only: https://www.cafonline.com/news/caf-invites-its-member-associations-mas-to-submit-bids-to-host-the-caf-africa-cup-of-nations-afcon-2028-2032-and-2036/
- EURO 2028 dates are the 2028 summer reference. Copa America 2028 and subsequent tournament dates not announced officially are marked **projected**, not official fixtures.

`tools/export_international_calendar.py` exports the events CSV or JSON, for example:

```bash
python tools/export_international_calendar.py --from-year 2026 --to-year 2050 --output reports/international_calendar.csv
```

No national-team result or tournament fixture is generated by this tool.

## Code integrations

- `data/future_seasons.py`: `normal_round_dates` produces a natural English fixture independently of FIFA; `round_dates` performs the separate blackout reflow and `generated_edition` records `original_kickoff` only if a date changed. Fixture IDs, opponents and rounds are never redrawn.
- `domain/calendar.py`: `reprogram_round_dates_for_blackouts` is the second-pass DP; ordinary `find_available_kickoff`/`reschedule_fixture_into_window` also accept blackout periods for manual reprogramming.
- `domain/world_runner.py`: optional international-calendar policy in generated worlds, all new league/cup/staged booking, explicit rescheduling, `international_window_violations()` and serialization. Legacy saves without this field still load.
- `domain/career.py`: generated future seasons receive an international calendar when the season is created.
- `ui_shell/calendar.py` + `frontend/portable/calendar.js`/`.css`: `/api/calendar` exposes `fifa_windows` and fixture rescheduling provenance. The frontend only renders a small globe per day (read-only).
- `tools/stress_world_calendar.py`: `--master-calendar` enables the post-generated FIFA overlay, and checks leagues, staged/cups and postponed fixtures. The synthetic stress world is NOT a real geographic competition database.

## Compatibility / known limits

1. **2026/27 now uses TC-E1.1 initial-season reflow.** Sporting fixtures are generated on their normal 2026/27 slots before an international overlay is applied. Premier whole matchweeks and individually slotted EFL fixtures are moved only as needed, preserving fixture IDs and original kickoffs. Completed encounters remain immutable. Cup and UEFA scheduling respects the same master from registration. See `docs/TC_E1_1_INITIAL_SEASON_REFLOW.md`. Previously saved world-result files are not automatically migrated; the disposable sandbox initializes a fresh world on restart.
2. Full real national-team simulations, call-up selection, player availability effects, early returns and international player fatigue are future features. The explicit registry is a contract, **not active match-day unavailability yet**.
3. Stadium/resource reservations and global cross-country real-club scheduling remain future integration work. Only club participation/rest and FIFA calendar block rules are currently enforced. The known staged-registration rollback defect is **not fixed by TC-E1** (reserved for TC-E2).
4. Post-2030 FIFA and many tournament dates are projections following the reference week's pattern, not future FIFA/CAF/AFC announcements; overrides are supported. Different league-season structures (calendar-year leagues) still need specific policies.
5. The current stress tests exercise cheap WorldSeasonRunner score resolution, not full national-team competition simulation or a multi-year persistent career with managed FastMatchSimulator every round.
6. Fixture reflow may move an *unblocked* matchweek to preserve matchweek ordering and club rest. `original_kickoff` shows what was changed.
