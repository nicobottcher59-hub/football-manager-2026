# Phase 4 - MatchSnapshot

## Goal

Phase 4 creates the immutable pre-match input consumed by the future Match Engine.
It does **not** simulate possessions, events, shots or results yet.

The snapshot is a frozen picture of the world at kickoff. Later changes to the
persistent `Player`, `PlayerState`, `Tactic` or familiarity objects must not alter
an already-created match input.

## Snapshot structure

### MatchSnapshot

Contains:

- stable match ID;
- deterministic random seed;
- full match context;
- frozen home-team snapshot;
- frozen away-team snapshot.

### TeamMatchSnapshot

Contains:

- stable club identity;
- submitted formation;
- exactly eleven frozen starter snapshots;
- frozen bench snapshots;
- frozen tactical configuration.

The builder validates that the submitted lineup belongs to the club and that the
tactic uses the same formation as the lineup.

### PlayerMatchSnapshot

Every selected player freezes:

- stable player ID and display identity;
- immutable player attributes;
- current fitness, fatigue, form, morale, confidence and match sharpness;
- injury/suspension state at snapshot time;
- complete positional familiarity values;
- complete known role/tactic familiarity values.

Starters additionally freeze:

- assigned formation slot and position;
- assigned role;
- effective individual instructions after overrides;
- position familiarity for the assignment;
- role familiarity;
- tactic familiarity;
- attribute-based role suitability.

Bench players intentionally have no starting slot or starting role. Their full
attributes and familiarity maps remain available for future substitution logic.

### TacticMatchSnapshot

Freezes:

- tactic identity/name;
- formation;
- preset origin as descriptive metadata;
- effective team instructions;
- every slot's role and effective player instructions;
- tactical style analysis.

The Match Engine must consume effective instructions. Preset labels and detected
style names are descriptive and never grant bonuses.

## Match context

### Competition context

The snapshot can carry the information needed by a future pre-match screen and by
context-sensitive simulation systems:

- competition and season identity;
- current stage/matchday label;
- an immutable standings table when relevant;
- optional cup round, leg and aggregate score before kickoff.

Competition rules are **not a user-configurable match setting** in this phase.
They belong to the competition model when that system is implemented.

### Date and time

`kickoff` is timezone-aware so the future UI can reliably show weekday, date and
local kickoff time.

### Stadium and pitch

The snapshot freezes:

- stadium identity/name/city;
- pitch length and width;
- surface type;
- current pitch condition.

Pitch dimensions and conditions are prepared as future Match Engine inputs, not
visual-only metadata.

### Weather

The frozen weather context includes:

- condition type;
- temperature;
- wind;
- humidity;
- precipitation intensity.

No player attribute is rewritten because of weather. Phase 5+ may let these values
modify the situations/actions generated by the engine.

### Referee

The referee snapshot includes tendencies for:

- strictness;
- contact tolerance;
- cards;
- penalties;
- playing advantage.

These are future action-resolution inputs. They are not direct team bonuses or
penalties.

### Kits

The selected home and away kits are frozen into the match context. Kit selection
is primarily presentation data and does not change footballing ability.

Kit assets remain decoupled through IDs / optional asset keys so a later frontend
or data pack can render them however it wants.

## Immutability boundary

The Phase 4 service converts mutable world/domain state into immutable match data.
For example, after a snapshot is built:

- changing a player's fitness does not change the match snapshot;
- improving positional or tactical familiarity does not change it;
- changing team tempo or another tactical instruction does not change it.

A new snapshot must be built to represent a new kickoff state.

## Determinism

`MatchSnapshot.seed` is part of the immutable input. The future simulation contract
is:

> same MatchSnapshot + same engine version = reproducible simulation

The actual seeded Match Engine is implemented after this phase.

## Frontend separation

Phase 4 is backend/domain work only. The future frontend may completely change how
this information is presented without changing the simulation contract.

Examples:

- pre-match cards;
- kit selector;
- league table / cup context panel;
- weather and referee presentation;
- tactical pitch editor;
- drag-and-drop player positioning.

The UI should edit world/pre-match configuration; once kickoff is confirmed, the
builder freezes that state into `MatchSnapshot`.

## Tests

Phase 4 adds tests for:

- complete match construction;
- starters and bench data;
- role/instruction/familiarity freezing;
- snapshot independence from later source mutations;
- lineup/tactic formation compatibility;
- kit ownership validation;
- home/away validation;
- timezone-aware kickoff;
- standings validation;
- cup aggregate context;
- referee/weather validation;
- deterministic seed validation.
