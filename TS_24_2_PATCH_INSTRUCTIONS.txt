TS-24.2 - PROFESSIONAL CONTRACTS AND CLUB FINANCES
INCREMENTAL UPDATE - NOT THE FULL GAME

Required base: TS-24.1 Player Market Valuation installed.
Python-only runtime; Node/npm is not needed to install or play.

1. Close the game AND its Python server.
2. Back up the complete project and your FULL SQLite career save.
3. Extract this ZIP into the existing project folder containing
   INICIAR_INTERFAZ_WINDOWS.bat. Preserve folders and replace files.
4. Verify the incremental package:
   python tools/verify_ts242_economy_package.py
5. Test the economy and contract code:
   python -m pytest -ra tests/test_ts_24_2_contracts_finances.py
   (the full-save tests can take longer than unit tests.)
6. Optional read-only financial calibration:
   python tools/audit_ts242_economy.py
7. Run INICIAR_INTERFAZ_WINDOWS.bat, refresh the browser with Ctrl+F5.
8. Open Finances: view transfer budget, bank cash, wage budget, committed
   payroll, individual professional contracts, renewal offers and monthly
   history. Contract details also appear in Squad and club/player views.

WHAT CHANGES
- One actual persisted professional salary and contract per registered
  first-team player, plus lower youth agreements in U17/U21.
- Actual club wage ceiling, reserved transfer allocation, monthly recurring
  simulated revenue, other operating costs, cash and payroll ledger for all
  92 clubs, using the SAME formulas for manager and AI.
- Signed weekly wage is FIXED until a renewal or a new agreement. Changes in
  OVR, ACT or temporary fitness DO NOT automatically change the wage.
- Manager may offer a renewal from Finances: 1 to 5 years (restricted for
  veterans), salary demand and board ceiling apply. Invalid offers fail
  without changing the saved contract or cash.
- AI clubs independently renew expiring deals within their own wage limits.
- Monthly wages/receipts debit/credit operating cash exactly once, with
  pro-rated initial month, and complete history for existing wage changes.
- New careers begin with coherent contracts/budgets; compatible TS-24.1
  careers get a no-retroactive-charge finance register at migration.
- Promotion from U17/U21 to the first team creates a pro deal and retains
  the same player ID; youth retirement/release removes old contract records.
- Budget and recurring simulated revenue adapt on new season promotion and
  relegation. No fabricated ticket receipts or completed transfers are added.

NOT IMPLEMENTED IN TS-24.2
- Buying or selling professional players; loan agreements; agents and their
  negotiated clauses; player consent to changing club.
- Real match-day gate receipts / television payments / actual costs by
  category. Initial revenues are transparent fiction calibrated to this
  simulator's opening world and will be upgraded in later finance work.
- Free-agent and contract expiry departures. Expired contracts use visibly
  flagged *paid interim retention*, not a silent salary cut or unpaid labor.
- Moving the transfer budget into wages manually (future update).
- Youth scouting allocation is still separate from club operating cash.

SAVE AND COMPATIBILITY
- Full SQLite manager save graph becomes version 13; TS-24.1 v12 compatible
  careers migrate on load. Loading does NOT overwrite the old file; the
  change to v13 is persisted when the user saves.
- Older app releases may not understand a v13 save. KEEP YOUR BACKUP.
- This ZIP does NOT ship personal save files.
